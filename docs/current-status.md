# Current 33-family checkpoint status

This consolidated source tree contains **33 distinct circuit-design families**: six pilots plus 27 experimental families. The count comes from the named tasks accepted by the public reset/step environments and JSON-lines servers below, not from directories, variants or example scripts. The [current machine-readable inventory](../evidence/current-inventory.json) enumerates every local public family API. The historical v37 inventory referred to below is not present in this source snapshot. All **27 experimental family APIs** have task-specific conditional reward rules; block quantization now has fixed proxy gates and a conditional score. The historical v36 inventory (not included in this checkpoint) records its earlier measurement-only status. A candidate can still be correct but reward-ineligible. The three [`quality` profiles](../chip_rl/quality/spec.py) for dot, tensor and TLB are extra scored configurations of existing families, **not three more families**. The supplemental [`history.check`](../chip_rl/history/evaluate.py) modes for CRC and retirement are **two measurement-only verification modes**, not agent tasks or additional rewards.

The newer family reports are separate, finite qualifications: [integer RMS](integer-rms-v1-phase2.md) and its [comb-root variant](integer-rms-serial-comb-root-phase2.md), [block RLE](block-rle-v1-phase2.md), [block FOR](block-for-v1-phase2.md) with its [shiftpack variant](block-for-shiftpack-phase2.md), [BF16 quantization](bf16-quant-v1-phase2.md), and [Hadamard transforms](hadamard-int8-v1-phase2.md). The [post-Hadamard full source unit suite](../evidence/hadamard-int8-v1/integration-verification.json) passed 240/240; its installed-wheel import and cache-only direct/JSONL API smokes passed with no fresh tool runs. This does **not** constitute a fresh 33-family public-API simulation sweep. The [BF16 compact evidence](../evidence/bf16-quant-v1/summary.json) records four scored calls, both references on all five N values, correct and qualified with zero reward; its 6,291,456 zero-mismatch comparisons are independent host arithmetic checks, not exhaustive RTL checks. Three N=4 arithmetic mutants compiled and synthesized, then failed source and mapped numerical checks. No BF16 formal, physical, model-accuracy, ISA-conformance or trained-RL claim follows. The [Hadamard frozen qualification](../evidence/hadamard-int8-v1/qualification.json) records both references correct in both splits across all five N values. Serial qualifies at reward zero. Stage-parallel fails reward qualification only at N=16, where its generic-cell ratio is 27766/5000 = 5.5532 above the fixed 4× gate; it has zero reward despite N=16 II 6 versus serial 34 and lower completion-cycle ratios (0.192 development, 0.184 heldout). Three predeclared arithmetic controls failed source/mapped numerical comparison after synthesis and compilation. Hadamard has no formal, physical or trained-RL result. The [bounded N=4 integer RMS physical reference comparison](rms-physical-reference-v1.md) completed both GDS routes at a fixed 100 ns Nangate45 constraint, but both 30-minute KLayout reference-deck DRC runs timed out incomplete. Its 39-vector source/mapped checks were pre-route; there was no LVS, SDF or post-route equivalence. Scoring eligibility, a positive reward, RTL/mapped correctness, and physical PPA are distinct claims. See the [machine-readable inventory](../evidence/current-inventory.json) for which evidence files actually exist in this snapshot.

For each new family, a scored result requires verified candidate and serial-baseline metrics at exactly N={1,2,4,8,16}; area must be ≤4× baseline and completion cycles ≤ baseline at every N. Saturated acceptance II must be ≤2N+24 for integer RMS, ≤N+6 for block RLE, and ≤2N+6 for block FOR; BF16 also requires ≤N+6. Hadamard requires II ≤(N/2)log2(N)+6, with N a power of two. A correct design failing any gate earns zero and is not reward-qualified. Qualified balanced reward is max(0, 1 − exp(mean(log(area ratio × cycle ratio))/2)); it may also be zero. These rules come from the current family scoring modules and remain generic-cell/cycle proxies.

## Interpretation and remaining limits

A baseline smoke confirms that a default reference works through a public entry point on the checked finite workload. It does not repeat each report's alternate-design, mutant, heldout or formal matrix. The family contracts and reports distinguish correctness from service eligibility, cache identity, reward scope and proxy limits. There is no universal proof, physical power result, trained-policy improvement, or same-UID OS isolation for arbitrary untrusted candidates in this checkpoint. The one bounded N=4 routed reference comparison is not DRC-cleared or signoff and does not physically qualify any family reward.
