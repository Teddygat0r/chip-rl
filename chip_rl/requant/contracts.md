# Exact integer requantization v24.1

N=1..4 lanes. Fixed ports: clk/rst/in_valid/in_ready/out_valid/out_ready, in_data[191:0], in_mask[3:0], out_data[31:0]. Lane i accumulator occupies in_data[32*i+:32] as signed INT32. Shared unsigned scale is [143:128], shift [149:144] is 0..47, signed INT8 zero point is [157:150]. Bits [191:158] and lanes i>=N are ignored, including their mask bits. Each active lane computes exact product, divides by 2**shift rounded to nearest with ties to even, adds zero point, then clamps to [-128,127]. Byte i in output is the signed two's-complement result; masked/unused bytes are zero. Invalid shifts are outside generated workload domain; action cannot change the domain. No tolerance or float arithmetic.

One response per accepted request, in order; producer holds offered data until accepted, output valid/data stable under backpressure. Synchronous active-high reset cancels outstanding work and suppresses ready/valid during reset. Registered minimum response latency one edge. Accepted work must complete within 8*N+16 cycles under the scored periodic consumer schedule (three blocked of eleven); whole workload bound prevents refusing input indefinitely. Long unscored stall/cancellation probes exclude intentionally blocked time from that deadline. Reserved inputs are poisoned on idle clocks. Workload includes >1024 consecutive requests without reset.

Development N2,4 and heldout N1,3 use unchanged candidate source and separate distribution/traffic generators. Fixed parallel baseline. Resource cap 45000 generic cells, 160 combinational levels. Eligibility requires per-case cycle ratio<=4 and offered-latency ratio<=6; correctness is separate. Reward for eligible designs is 1-.5*tanh(.6*ln(geomean cell ratio)+.3*ln(geomean cycle ratio)+.1*ln(geomean depth ratio)). Ineligible or incorrect reward zero. Fresh private seeds per evaluation; trusted replay seed only. No physical timing/power, general formal or OS isolation claim.
